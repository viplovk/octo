How I Work

The process

01
Observe
Everything starts with looking closely — at material behaviour, at how light hits a surface, at the decision that made something feel right.
02
Constrain
I set a tight parameter — one ink, one tool, one substrate — and work until the constraint becomes the work. Limitation is generative.
03
Make
Physical process first. I print, carve, press, scan. The digital layer comes after — to sharpen what already exists, never to replace it.
04
Refine
I repeat until the graphic structure holds under noise. The work is done when texture and clarity coexist without compromise.
